Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what you explicitly provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling you to manage your financial records. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to delivering the app's core functionality.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access. Your expense data is private to your account and is not visible to other users. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app, but these providers are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our site. We will do our best to respond promptly.
